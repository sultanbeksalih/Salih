Courses · Курстар

Pick a filter, then press Details (Толық) on any card. Prices are monthly, in tenge ₸.

Market reference (Kazinform): ENT prep in Aktobe starts at about 65,000 ₸/month for a full multi-subject course; some offers reach 150,000 ₸. Private lessons start around 2,000 ₸/hour.

Addresses · Мекенжайлар

Every point on one list. Buttons open Google Maps / 2GIS or start a call.

Exam files · Емтихан файлдары

Real exam files from official sources (NCT, IDP, British Council). Press Download (Жүктеу), the PDF opens, then save it.

Score calculator · Калькулятор

Pick an exam, move the sliders, see your total. (Емтиханды таңдап, жалғастырыңыз.)

Choose subjects · Пәндер таңдау

ENT = 3 compulsory subjects + 2 profile subjects = 140 points. Tap your future field to see the usual profile pair. (Болашақ бағытты таңдаңыз.)

These are typical pairs. The allowed pairs depend on your specialty and the year, so confirm on testcenter.kz and with your university.
